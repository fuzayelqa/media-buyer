02 / METHOD

Small bets. Hard tests.

  1. Research Study the offer, the audience, and what competitors already run.
  2. Launch small Low budget first. I'd rather lose a little to learn fast.
  3. Test hard Creatives, audiences, placements. Change one thing at a time.
  4. Kill losers No attachment. If it doesn't pay, it stops.
  5. Scale winners Raise budgets in steps and watch whether ROI holds.
  6. Report Spend, revenue, profit. Plain numbers, no spin.

05 / ABOUT

Accountant first. Media buyer now.

I studied Economics (BSS) and began my professional career in Accounting, where I developed strong financial discipline, analytical thinking, and attention to detail. I later gained experience as a Bank Teller before transitioning into Visa Processing and Marketing.

2025, I moved into Digital Marketing as a Media Buyer at Anolipi Digital, specializing in Search Arbitrage and Meta Ads Campaign Management. My focus is on maximizing ROI, maintaining low CPC, improving RPC, optimizing campaign performance, and making data driven decisions.

Alongside my marketing experience, I have developed expertise in Quality Assurance (SQA), including manual testing, automation testing with Python and Pytest, Selenium, API testing with Postman, and performance testing with JMeter.

My accounting background taught me that every taka spent must have a purpose. My QA experience taught me that every system and tracking process must be reliable. I bring these skills together in media buying by focusing on accurate tracking, data analysis, budget optimization, campaign quality, and profitable results.
